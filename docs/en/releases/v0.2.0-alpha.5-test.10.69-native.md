# OMSI Map Studio v0.2.0-alpha.5-test.10.69-native

## Test 10.69 — junctions follow the local terrain plane

This preview improves procedural junctions placed on sloped roads.

- the planner samples real terrain in four directions around each junction;
- sampling distance follows the physical junction footprint;
- longitudinal terrain slope becomes **Pitch**;
- lateral terrain slope becomes **Bank**;
- calculations respect the shared asset's canonical rotation;
- Pitch/Bank are clamped to **±12°** to avoid extreme tilt from noisy or broken terrain;
- junction center height still comes from the real terrain sample;
- junctions connected to bridge/tunnel/layer segments do not receive ground-derived tilt, avoiding mixed structural levels;
- the OMSI writer now receives real Pitch and Bank instead of fixed zero values;
- no `.terrain` file is modified;
- Renderer regression coverage validates a crossing on a 10% terrain grade.

This reduces junction surfaces floating above or cutting through sloped terrain.
