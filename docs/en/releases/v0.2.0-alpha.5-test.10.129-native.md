# OMSI Map Studio v0.2.0-alpha.5-test.10.129-native

## Test 10.129 — original OMSI 2 asset family catalog

This build uses the public list of objects and splines present in a clean OMSI 2 installation as a reference:
https://reboot.omsi-webdisk.de/wiki/entry/46-list-of-all-original-objects-and-splines/

The implementation does not copy the complete list. Core stores stable family rules and still requires every asset to exist in the user's local OMSI installation.

### Roads

- recognizes original `str_*`, `Hstr_*`, `DEMO_LHT_str_*` and `str_ch_*` families;
- recognizes `sdwk_*` as sidewalks/paths and `rail_*` as rail splines;
- original invisible splines are not used as visible automatic roads;
- between physically equivalent candidates, an original OMSI spline is preferred over an addon asset;
- real SLI geometry, surfaces and `[path]` data are still validated before use.

### Junctions, signs and crossings

- `Kreuz_MC` and `Kreuz_RUE` are recognized as original junction families;
- `Einm_*` and `Kreuz_*` no longer depend on generic filename words;
- `Zebra_*` and `Crosswalk_*` are separated from junction objects;
- `Ampel_*` is recognized as original traffic-signal content;
- `VZ_*`/Verkehrszeichen is recognized as traffic-sign content.

Automatic junction SCO placement remains conservative: the catalog improves identification, but Map Studio does not force a stock junction until connection points and fit are validated.

### Street furniture and transit

Automatic reconstruction now recognizes and prefers original families for:

- `Busstop_*`, `hst_*` and `Wartehaus_*` stops/shelters;
- `strlt_*`, `peitschenleuchte*`, `Gaslight_*`, `neonlight_*` and `sodiumlight_*` lighting;
- `muelleinmer_*` waste baskets;
- `bollard_*` bollards;
- original traffic signs and signals;
- `Zebra_*` crossings.

Stock `Trees_MC` and `Buildings_*` families are also identified for classification and future more specific reconstruction matching.

### Regression coverage

The test suite covers representative real original-family paths and verifies classification, junction/crosswalk separation, original-road priority over equivalent addon splines, and original street-furniture selection.
