# OMSI Map Studio v0.2.0-alpha.5-test.10.71-native

## Test 10.71 — structural geometry for elevated and underground junctions

This preview improves the physical appearance of junctions that are not at ground level.

- `MapStudioJunctionSpec` now distinguishes **Ground**, **Bridge**, and **Tunnel**;
- structural type is included in the generated asset signature/hash, preventing a ground junction asset from being reused for an elevated crossing;
- **Bridge** junctions gain an approximately **30 cm** underside slab and structural edge skirt;
- **Tunnel** junctions gain a structural ceiling roughly **4.2 m** above the road surface;
- structural surfaces reuse the existing gray junction material with no new external texture dependency;
- the planner infers Bridge/Tunnel from homogeneous connected `bridge/tunnel/layer` state;
- mixed-grade junctions remain Ground instead of incorrectly joining incompatible levels;
- Test 10.70 structural elevation remains preserved;
- Core validates the extra geometry and Renderer validates inferred structural type;
- no `.terrain` file is modified.

This prevents elevated junctions from looking like a zero-thickness asphalt plate and gives tunnel intersections a real central ceiling.
