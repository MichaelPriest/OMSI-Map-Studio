# OMSI Map Studio v0.2.0-alpha.5-test.2-native

**ALPHA 5 TEST 2 — Native Feature Preview** build of the WinUI 3 + Direct3D 11 architecture.

This is a **validation prerelease**. PR #3 remains DRAFT and unmerged while this build carries the latest native branch work for testing on real OMSI maps.

## Main changes since Test 1

### Native OMSI water

- real reading of the `tile_X_Y.map.water` sidecar;
- strict validation of the canonical 20-byte binary format;
- all four water-surface corner heights are preserved;
- conservative recognition/editing of the `[water]` section in tile `.map` files;
- Direct3D 11 water mesh kept outside the picking buffer;
- native active-tile water editor;
- individual editing of all four corners;
- level-all-corners action using their average height;
- safe create/update/remove operations with backup and rollback;
- immediate snapshot reload after save/remove;
- malformed water sidecars no longer make the map unreadable.

### Procedural roads and junctions

- trace smoothing before preview and persistence;
- safe degree-2 auto-linking across separate trace fragments;
- degree-3/4 nodes remain junctions and are never linked through automatically;
- preview and persistence use the same smoothed geometry;
- original Road Kit junction generation remains transactional with rollback.

### OSM and buildings

- safe outer building multipolygon relation support;
- relation-consumed ways are not duplicated;
- relations with `inner` rings remain rejected to avoid filling courtyards/holes incorrectly;
- Hip/Pyramidal roofs on simple convex footprints with three or more sides;
- planar Shed/Skillion roofs on simple irregular or concave footprints;
- Gable remains restricted to quadrilateral footprints;
- additional irregular-roof tests.

### OSM vegetation

- tree/shrub nodes;
- tree rows and hedges;
- wood/forest/scrub areas;
- safe outer multipolygons;
- selection of real SCO assets already indexed in the library;
- terrain-aware preview and batch persistence with backup;
- production never fabricates vegetation assets.

### Building Studio and AI

- multiple reference images for building analysis;
- up to 8 images handled by the compatible provider;
- provider-neutral architecture remains in place;
- current real host adapters: `openai-compatible`, `ollama`, and `lmstudio`;
- AI returns structured suggestions only; final SCO/O3D generation remains local to Map Studio.

### Native editor

- Tracks, Trips, Stops, StationLinks, and Lines/Tours remain integrated in WinUI;
- traffic paths, traffic rules, and traffic-light programs remain available in the native workflow;
- backups and conservative validation remain mandatory for writes.

## Validate in this Alpha

1. Open Grundorf and at least one larger map.
2. Verify objects, splines, terrain, and materials still load correctly.
3. On the active tile, open **Water** and create a surface with four different heights.
4. Test leveling, save, close/reopen the map, and verify persistence.
5. Remove water and verify both `[water]` and `.map.water` are removed while a backup is retained.
6. Test procedural roads with separate segments and junction nodes.
7. Test OSM import on a map copy, including buildings and vegetation.
8. Test Building Studio with multiple references.
9. Inspect `.mapstudio-backups` before and after destructive operations.

## Known limitations

- this remains a validation Alpha;
- water uses the real OMSI four-corner surface; advanced visual water effects may still evolve;
- OSM building/vegetation relations with inner rings remain blocked for safety;
- Proton Bus, LOTUS, and other simulators do not yet have complete operational adapters;
- real subscription/license enforcement remains disabled in Development Preview;
- addon-specific OMSI materials may still require refinements.
