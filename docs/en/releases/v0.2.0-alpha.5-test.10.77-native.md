# OMSI Map Studio v0.2.0-alpha.5-test.10.77-native

## Test 10.77 — adaptive retaining-wing depth and tunnel-mouth cover

This preview deepens the integration between the tunnel portal, slope and structural tunnel lining.

- each side of the mouth now also samples terrain continuity along the longitudinal direction;
- left/right retaining-wing depth is resolved independently;
- when terrain keeps rising away from the mouth, that side is automatically extended deeper;
- depths are quantized in **0.50 m** increments to preserve asset reuse/caching;
- depth limits prevent wings that are too short or excessively large;
- asset names now include `DL` and `DR` in addition to width/heights, providing a complete geometry signature;
- the portal gains an upper **roof apron** that continues the structure over the mouth and reduces the visual seam with tunnel lining;
- the apron uses the larger depth required by either side;
- terrain probes remain protected against unloaded tile boundaries;
- the system still does not modify `.terrain`;
- Core/Renderer regressions validate asymmetric depth, asset signatures and full terrain preservation.

Next steps: structural drainage, optional internal lighting and entrance/exit detailing.
